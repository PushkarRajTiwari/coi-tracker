# COI Tracker: Week 1 validation (week of 2026-10-08)

## 1. The headline: the "gap" is already filled

The brief assumes the market is spreadsheets on one side and $800–$10,000+/yr platforms on the other. That was true a few years ago. It is not true now. Several of the small tools (COI Compass, TrackMyVendor, SubDoc) already ship the exact MVP feature set: AI ACORD 25 extraction with review, magic-link vendor upload with no login, 30/14/7 reminders, pass/fail against requirements, CSV/PDF export.

**What this means**
- Demand is real. Ten companies selling the same thing is proof people pay for it. Validation this week is not "does anyone want this?"
- Price and "simple" are not a differentiator on their own. Our planned Free (5) / Starter $49 (25 vendors) is worse than TrackMyVendor's free 25 and $39 unlimited.
- The real question for week 1: **can you get in front of buyers, and is there one angle where you are clearly better?** If you cannot answer that by the end of week 2, I'd move the kill check from day 90 to day 30 rather than spend three months building a me-too.

But every competitor above is already publishing "best COI software" comparison posts, and a new domain will not rank for these in 90 days. **SEO is a month 4–12 channel, not a first-customer channel.** "Free coi tracking software" (140/mo) is also a warning: a meaningful share of searchers want free, and incumbents give it.

The free checker page is still the right lead magnet, but target the informational long tail ("how to read a certificate of insurance", "what does additional insured mean on a COI", "ACORD 25 waiver of subrogation") where CPC is near zero and competition is low.

## 3. Where you might actually win (pick one this week)

1. **The insurance-agent channel (strongest).** Agents issue COIs all day and their small contractor clients ask them "is this sub's cert OK?". A tool an agent can hand to clients (co-branded, agent gets a referral cut) is a distribution edge none of the solo-founder tools above lean on. This is already in your plan; make it the main bet, not a side channel.
2. **Property managers / HOA managers with recurring vendors** rather than GCs. Most small tools above say "for GCs". Vendor lists are stable, renewals are annual, and PMs churn less than project-based GCs. Narrow copy wins over "contractors and property managers".
3. **Email-forward-only workflow.** "Forward the cert to you@inbox.yourapp.com, done." Competitors lead with upload/magic links. This is a UX edge, not a moat; weakest of the three.

My recommendation: lead the landing page with **small property managers**, and spend your interview time on **2 insurance agents + 3 property managers**. GC copy can come later.

## 4. Pricing: revised recommendation

The planned grid sits above TrackMyVendor and SubDoc while offering less. Revised to sit just under COI File / COI Compass, above the near-free tools, with more vendors per tier:

| Plan | Price | Vendors | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 10 | Enough to try with real vendors; extraction cost is cents per cert |
| Starter | $29/mo | 50 | |
| Pro | $59/mo | 150 | Audit export, requirement templates per trade |
| Team | $119/mo | 400 | Multi-user; can be hidden until someone asks |

Annual = 2 months free. **Founding pilot: 50% off any plan, locked for 12 months** (Starter becomes $14.50, Pro $29.50). This keeps your $29 founding number for the plan most pilots will need.

Honest MRR math: at ~$40 average revenue per account, $500 MRR needs ~13 paying customers and $1,500 needs ~38. Achievable by month 12 only if one channel (agents) works. The $2,500+ upside case needs a niche where you're the obvious choice.

Costs this week: domain ~$12. Everything else free.

**Exit criteria for week 1 (be strict):**
- Go: ≥1 agent open to referring clients, or ≥2 prospects say "I'd pay $15–30/mo for a pilot" *after* hearing free tools exist.
- Narrow: interest exists but everyone is happy with a free tier → reposition (agent tool / PM niche) before writing code.
- Pivot early: no one engaged → test the bank-statement converter now rather than at day 90.

## 6. Interview script (15 minutes)

1. How do you track subs'/vendors' insurance certificates today? Show me if you can.
2. How many vendors? How many certs expire in a typical month?
3. Last time a cert lapsed or was wrong, what happened? What did it cost?
4. Have you looked at any tools? Which, and why didn't you keep using it? (Listen for TrustLayer/bcs free tiers.)
5. If this took 10 minutes to set up and chased renewals for you, what would that be worth monthly?
6. For agents: how often do clients ask you to check a sub's certificate? Would you hand them a tool with your name on it?

Do not pitch until question 6. Write down exact words; they become landing page copy.

## 7. Changes to the 30-day plan

- **Week 2** stays (extraction prototype + checker), but only start if week 1 hits "Go" or "Narrow".
- **Week 3**: build only what the interviews asked for; skip Team plan, multi-user and per-trade templates unless requested.
- **Week 4**: 3–5 pilots is right. Directory listings yes; Product Hunt later (it rarely brings SMB construction/PM buyers).
- **Kill check**: keep "5 paying by day 90", add an earlier checkpoint: **≥3 committed pilots (verbal yes + email) by day 30**, or pivot.
