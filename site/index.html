Questions

Do you verify coverage with the insurance carrier?
No. We track and organize the certificates your vendors send and check what's written on them against your requirements. A certificate is not proof of coverage; for that, contact the issuing agent or carrier.

Where are my documents stored?
In private, encrypted cloud storage. Files are never public and links expire.

Do my vendors need an account?
No. They get an email with a one-click upload link.
